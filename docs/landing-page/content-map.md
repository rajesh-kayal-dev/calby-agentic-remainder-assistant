# Calby Landing Page Content Map

This document maps out the section-by-section copywriting, structural elements, visual assets, and message hierarchy for the redesigned landing page.

---

## Central Message
> **"Tell Calby what needs to get done."**  
> *Your conversational personal assistant for the things you need to remember, organize, schedule and follow up.*

---

## Section-by-Section Content Map

### 01. Navbar
- **Brand**: Calby logo icon + "Calby" brand text.
- **Nav Links**: Product (`#product`), Capabilities (`#capabilities`), Intelligence (`#intelligence`), Reports (`#reports`), Integrations (`#integrations`), Security (`#security`).
- **Actions**: "Log in" (`/sign-in`), "Get started" (`/sign-in` with lime button & arrow).

### 02. Hero
- **Headline**: "Tell Calby what needs to get done."
- **Gradient Highlight**: "Your conversational personal assistant."
- **Supporting Copy**: "Calby remembers the details, keeps your tasks and money organized, manages your calendar, and follows up when something is still pending."
- **CTAs**: Primary: "Get started" (`/sign-in`), Secondary: "See how it works" (`#product-demo`).
- **Hero Visual**: Realistic dual-chat bubble mockup featuring money tracking (Rahul ₹350 with itemized Books & Food) and scheduled reminder ("Remind me tomorrow").

### 03. Audience Ticker (`TrustedBy`)
- **Headline**: "BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO JUGGLE MULTIPLE RESPONSIBILITIES"
- **Ticker Items**: Freelancers, Busy Professionals, Small Business Owners, Consultants, Product Managers, Founders, Remote Teams, Developers.

### 04. Real Product Demo (`#product-demo`)
- **Headline**: "One assistant. Everything connected."
- **Sub-headline**: "Calby connects your calendar, tasks, money, reminders, and contacts into a unified conversational workspace."
- **Visual Mockup Layout**:
  - **Left Sidebar**: Calby Navigation (Assistant, Calendar, Tasks, Money, Reminders, Contacts, Reports).
  - **Center Panel**: Conversation thread showing multi-step intelligence.
  - **Right Panel**: Live context drawer showing Today's Schedule (10:00 Standup, 14:00 Client Call) & Pending Items (Rahul ₹350, 2 tasks).

### 05. What Calby Can Handle (`#capabilities`)
- **Headline**: "Everything you need to stay on top of your day."
- **6 Clean Cards**:
  1. **Calendar**: "Find time, schedule meetings, and reschedule events."
  2. **Tasks**: "Keep track of the things you need to finish."
  3. **Money**: "Remember who owes you money and what it was for."
  4. **Reminders**: "Tell Calby once. Let it remember when you need it."
  5. **Contacts**: "Keep people and their pending work together."
  6. **Reports**: "Turn your activity into useful reports and send them where they belong."

### 06. Conversational Intelligence (`#intelligence`)
- **Headline**: "You don't need to learn how to use Calby."
- **Supporting Copy**: "Just tell it what you need, the way you would tell a person."
- **Scannable Chat Cards**:
  - *Money*: "Rahul owes me 500 for books." → "Added ₹500 to Rahul's pending balance for Books."
  - *Tasks*: "Add buy printer paper to my work list." → "Added it to your work list."
  - *Calendar*: "Find me 30 minutes with Sarah tomorrow." → "I found a free 30-minute slot tomorrow at 2:30 PM."
  - *Unified Contact Intelligence*: "What's still pending with Rahul?" → Rahul card showing ₹350 pending (Books ₹200, Food ₹150) + 2 tasks + 1 reminder.

### 07. Memory + Follow-Up (`#memory`)
- **Headline**: "Tell Calby once. It keeps track."
- **Supporting Copy**: "Calby maintains context over time so you never have to re-explain who owes what or what was left pending."
- **Timeline Comparison**:
  - **Day 1**: "Rahul owes me ₹200 for the book." → "Got it. Added ₹200 to Rahul's pending balance."
  - **Days Later**: "What is pending with Rahul?" → "Rahul has ₹200 pending for the book."

### 08. Reports + Automation (`#reports`)
- **Headline**: "Turn your work into a report."
- **Supporting Copy**: "Generate summaries of your tasks, money ledger, and reminders, then export or schedule them automatically."
- **Visual Report Digest**: Monthly summary card (Tasks completed/pending, Money pending/collected, Reminders).
- **Delivery & Export Channels**: Google Docs, Google Sheets, Gmail, Telegram, WhatsApp.
- **Scheduled Automation**: "Every Monday at 9 AM, send me my pending report." → "Done. I'll generate your pending report every Monday at 9 AM."

### 09. Integrations & LLM Provider Flexibility (`#integrations`)
- **Headline**: "Works with the tools you already use."
- **Verified Integrations**: Google Calendar, Gmail, Telegram, WhatsApp, Google Docs, Google Sheets.
- **LLM Provider Subsection**: "Bring your preferred AI provider." (OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, Ollama, OpenRouter, Perplexity).

### 10. Security & Privacy (`#security`)
- **Headline**: "Your data stays yours."
- **Verified Security Pillars**:
  - **Secure OAuth Connections**: Standard token management via Descope with zero token exposure.
  - **Strict User Isolation**: Every record and query strictly scoped to your authenticated account.
  - **Protected Credentials**: Provider API keys encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  - **Server-Side Authorization**: Tool executions strictly enforce server-side user context.

### 11. Real Use Cases (`#use-cases`)
- **Cards for**:
  - **Busy Professional**: "What's on my schedule tomorrow?"
  - **Freelancer**: "Rahul still owes me ₹2,000 for the redesign."
  - **Small Business Owner**: "Show me everything pending this month."
  - **Consultant**: "Send my monthly pending report to Gmail."
  - **Personal**: "Remind me to pick up the book tomorrow at 5 PM."

### 12. Final CTA
- **Headline**: "Stop keeping everything in your mind."
- **Supporting Copy**: "Tell Calby what needs to happen. Let it remember, organize, and follow up."
- **CTA Button**: "Get started" (`/sign-in`).

### 13. Footer
- **Brand info**, navigation links, resources, account links, copyright.
